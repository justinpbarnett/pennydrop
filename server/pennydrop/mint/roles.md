# Theme roles

- `ground`: the stage background. Text and marks must read against it.
- `ink`: primary text and strong strokes.
- `muted`: secondary text, captions, index numbers, quiet marks.
- `rule`: hairlines, outlines of inactive things, dividers.
- `fill`: the default surface of a panel, cell or card.
- `fill2`: a second surface for emphasis or alternate rows.
- `edge`: strokes of connectors, axes and outlines that should be visible but calm.
- `accent`: the one color a Theme uses to say "look here"; use sparingly.
- `active`: the thing being acted on right now.
- `compare`: things being compared or examined.
- `swap`: things being exchanged or moved.
- `done`: finished, settled or sorted things.
- `ok`: success.
- `warn`: caution, a near miss, a retry.
- `fault`: error, loss, a broken or overwritten thing.
- `highlight`: a soft emphasis wash behind something.
- `series1` to `series6`: six distinct colors that tell members of a set apart; use them in order.
